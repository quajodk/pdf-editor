### Technical Implementation Notes

**PDF Coordinate System:**
- Canvas: Origin at top-left, Y increases downward
- PDF: Origin at bottom-left, Y increases upward
- Conversion: `pdfY = pageHeight - canvasY - height`

**pdf-lib Rendering:**
- Text: `page.drawText()` with font embedding
- Rectangles: `page.drawRectangle()` with border
- Ellipses: `page.drawEllipse()` with center and radii
- Lines: `page.drawLine()` with start/end points
- Highlights: Rectangles with opacity
- Drawings: Series of connected `drawLine()` calls

**Limitations:**
- pdf-lib doesn't support arrow heads (drawn as regular lines)
- pdf-lib doesn't support smooth curves (pen strokes are segmented)
- Font embedding not yet implemented (uses default fonts)
